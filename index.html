Questions from advertisers

Good to know before you start.

Who can advertise on WebSwan Media Network?

Any licensed or established local business: realtors, insurance agents, mortgage brokers, electricians, plumbers, accountants, contractors, salons, restaurants and more. Doctors and attorneys have their own platform at ads.webswanmedianetwork.com.

What does my ad include?

A banner with your logo, message and button, plus a business profile with your services, service area, phone and a link to your own website or booking page.

I don't have a banner. Can you make one?

Yes. Send us your logo and what you want to promote, and we design the banner for you.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your category and placement. Text (415) 877-0687 for current pricing, or call (239) 667-0151 and Rachel will explain how to get listed.

Can you promise me new customers?

No one can honestly promise that. What we do is put your business in front of people who are already looking for your kind of service, and make it easy for them to reach you.

How do I get started?

Fill out the form on this page or call (239) 667-0151. We will talk about your business, pick a placement and get your banner live.
